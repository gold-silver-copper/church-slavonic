# Synodal gold oracle: the token comparison contract (normative)

This document is the single normative definition of how a token of the
Elizabeth Bible is compared against the Synodal engine. Every gate,
generator, and adjudication step uses these rules and no others. The
paradigm-side (Alypy) contract is a separate section to be added by that
workstream; this document covers the **token side**.

Source of truth: `data/intermediate/synodal/ponomar-elizabeth-bible-2026-08-09.jsonl`
(37,211 verse records, pinned and checksummed under `references/`).
Committed artifact: `data/synodal/gold_token_oracle.tsv`, generated by
`cargo xtask synodal-gold-oracle`.

## 1. Record selection and apparatus

The oracle enumerates every token of every **scriptural verse record**.
Two categories of non-scriptural material exist in the pinned source and
are handled explicitly:

- **Composite records** (`passage` beginning `Composite.` — 8 records):
  Ponomar lectionary composites. Each is a rubric heading (containing the
  Latin word `Composite`, feast rubrics, and reading titles) followed by
  verses duplicated from their home books. They are excluded from the
  oracle entirely; their scriptural content is already attested at its
  home references.
- **In-verse apparatus**, stripped before tokenization
  (`synodal_gold_oracle::strip_apparatus`):
  - parenthesized **zachalo markers** — `(Зача́ло N)` (138 records) and
    the abbreviated `(Заⷱ҇ N)` / `(Заⷱ҇)` (584 records). These are
    lectionary navigation, not verse text. A parenthetical is a zachalo
    marker iff its content starts with `Зач` or `За` + U+2DF1.
  - square-bracketed **cross-references** (e.g. `[Быт 17:15]`): always
    apparatus.

  All other parenthesized material — supplied readings such as `(и҆)`,
  `(же)`, `(є҆́сть)` — **is** verse text and is kept; the parentheses
  themselves are punctuation (§3) and never enter a token.

## 2. Tokenization and canonical normalization

Tokenization is the existing
`synodal_church_slavonic_dictionary::coverage::tokenize`: a token is a
maximal run of alphabetic characters, combining marks, the Cyrillic
thousands sign U+0482, and the combining Cyrillic letters U+2DE0–U+2DFF.
Everything else separates tokens.

The **canonical surface** of a token is its printed slice under Unicode
NFC (`synodal_gold_oracle::canonical_surface`). Both sides of every
comparison — oracle surface and engine output — are taken to NFC before
comparing. The existing lookup projections
(`normalize_lookup` / `normalize_lookup_accentless` in
`church-slavonic-orthography`) are the only additional projections any
comparison may use, and only where an equivalence class below says so.

## 3. Typography equivalence classes

These are the **only** permitted deviations from exact NFC string
equality. Anything not enumerated here is compared exactly — accents,
breathings, and positional letter choices (`є`/`е`, `ѡ`/`о`, `ѻ`, `ꙋ`)
included.

1. **Titlo expansion.** A surface printed under a titlo (U+0483) or with
   combining letters (U+2DE0–U+2DFF) compares against the contraction
   the abbreviation module (`synodal_church_slavonic::abbreviation`)
   produces for the engine's reading: the engine's expanded form is
   contracted for the attested cell and compared exactly against the
   printed surface. An unexpandable titlo surface is a gap of class
   `abbreviation-unexpanded`, never a silent pass.
2. **Verse-initial presentation.** The first token of a verse is printed
   capitalized; comparison of a verse-initial token is case-insensitive
   (via `char::to_lowercase` on both sides, as in `normalize_lookup`).
   Non-initial tokens compare case-sensitively.
3. **Initial-uk presentation.** The word-initial digraph `ᲂу` (U+1C82 +
   `у`), the monograph `ѹ`/`Ѹ` (U+0479/U+0478), the capitalized `Оу`,
   and the expanded `оу` are one presentation class of the same letter
   pair; both sides compare in presented form via the uk folds inside
   `normalize_lookup` (`fold_digraph_uk`, `fold_uk_monograph`) and
   `present_initial_uk_digraph`.
4. **Punctuation and versification exclusion.** Punctuation, digits,
   whitespace, verse numbers, and bracket/parenthesis characters are not
   part of any token (they never survive tokenization, §2) and are
   compared by no rule. Versification itself (the `passage` field) is
   trusted as given by the pinned source.

## 4. Non-lexical token classification

Non-lexical tokens are typed, never silently excluded
(`synodal_church_slavonic_dictionary::coverage::classify_non_lexical`).
The oracle records the tag in its `non_lexical` column; the gate checks
**classification stability** (the tag reproduces) instead of morphology
for these rows.

- `non-lexical:letter-numeral` — the surface parses as a canonical
  Church Slavonic letter-numeral (`CyrillicNumeral::parse`) **and** the
  analyzer produces no lexical analysis under the liturgical profile.
  A lexical analysis always outranks the numeral reading, because titlo
  contractions can be letter-for-letter identical with numerals.
  *Known instance:* `сн҃а` (86 attestations) is the contraction of
  `сы́на` "Son" but parses as с+н+а = 251; until its abbreviation review
  lands it carries this tag (matching the engine's own classification).
  When the review lands, the tag drops — a classification change the
  gate surfaces and the regenerated oracle records.
- `non-lexical:foreign-script` — the surface contains a non-Cyrillic
  alphabetic character (Latin, Greek). After Composite exclusion the
  pinned Bible source attests none; the class is defined because the
  witnesses and future sources do contain such insertions.

The tag vocabulary is closed; adding a kind is a change to this document.

## 5. The committed token oracle

`data/synodal/gold_token_oracle.tsv` — one row per distinct canonical
surface (58,467 types over 658,009 tokens; ~3.2 MB). Deterministic:
rows sort by surface byte order; regeneration is byte-identical.

Header block (all `#`-prefixed, in order): title, `generated-by`
(the exact generator command), `source`, `contract` (this document),
`reference-cap`, `rows` (data-row count), `body-sha256` (SHA-256 over
everything from the column-header line to end of file). Columns:

| column | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `surface` | canonical (NFC) printed surface type |
| `count` | attestation count across all scriptural records |
| `non_lexical` | empty, or the §4 kind (`letter-numeral`, `foreign-script`) |
| `references` | first attesting passages, deduplicated, capped at 8 (`reference-cap`); evidence pointers only — the count is authoritative |
| `confirmed_readings` | `;`-joined `lexeme_id\|cell` pairs from the reviewed `synodal-evaluate` rows (`data/synodal/evaluation.tsv`) whose `expected_printed` equals this surface — the 2,499 curated evaluations folded in as confirmed readings for the gate to consume directly |

The cap trims only reference lists, never types: every distinct surface
of the Bible is a row.

### Staleness contract

`cargo xtask synodal-gold-oracle --check`:

- with the intermediate source present (local): regenerate and compare
  byte-for-byte; any difference fails with instructions to rerun the
  generator;
- without it (CI has no `data/intermediate`): validate the committed
  artifact self-consistently — generator header present, declared `rows`
  equals the actual data-row count, declared `body-sha256` matches the
  body.

The check is not yet wired into `check-structure`; the forthcoming
`synodal-gold` gate will own that wiring.

## 6. Witnesses and source-defect adjudication

Two independent digitizations of the same Bible serve as witnesses, not
oracles:

- `data/intermediate/synodal/crosswire-csl-elizabeth-1.5.2.jsonl`
  (36,000 records; modernized civil spelling, e.g. "В начале сотвори
  Бог небо и землю" — usable for lexical/word-presence adjudication,
  not orthographic detail);
- `data/intermediate/synodal/wikisource-church-slavonic-bible-2026-08-09.jsonl`
  (36,919 records; full Church Slavonic orthography, community
  transcription).

Both share the verse-record schema of the pinned source (`passage`,
`normalized_spelling`, provenance fields). They are loaded lazily and
streamed (`synodal_gold_oracle::witness_token_surfaces`); no witness
artifact is committed.

**Adjudication rule (two-of-three):** when the engine disagrees with
Ponomar on a surface, the corresponding verse is loaded from both
witnesses. If at least two witnesses side against Ponomar's reading, the
row is a **source defect**: it moves to
`data/synodal/gold_source_defects.tsv` with both witness readings
recorded as evidence, and is excluded from the gap. If the witnesses
split or are unavailable for the verse, the row stays in the gap as
`witness-conflict` pending a human call. The gate re-validates every
ledger row's two-witness disagreement on each run, so the ledger cannot
quietly absorb engine bugs.

Ledger columns: `passage`, `ponomar_surface`, `adjudicated_surface`,
`crosswire_reading`, `wikisource_reading`, `note`. The ledger starts
empty; rows enter only through the rule above.
