## Standing rules

- The lexicon holds lexical facts; the class tables hold paradigms; the
  crate holds rules that are true of the language. A form a class
  produces is never stored; a stem a class can derive is never stored;
  an accent a paradigm names is never listed. Removing lines and list
  entries is the measure of a rule's success; a rule that removes none
  is a failure and is not kept.
- Gate on measured numbers, never on self-consistency. Every number is
  printed by `cargo xtask eval`, `check-treebank`, `score-disambiguation`,
  an import report or a census; record before and after, and record a
  number that went down with its reason.
- The round-trip invariant: `check-treebank` at zero mismatches over
  34,470 verses; every auto-lifted leaf names every cell of its lexeme
  that prints the token; syncretism is the set, homonymy is `:amb n`, a
  narrowing carries `:by` with the rule or model that made it and is
  scored against the hand overlay outside the invariant.
- The library depends on `unicode-normalization` only; anything that
  learns from a corpus lives in the tools or tagger crate.
- Class tables are generated (`scripts/legend-adj-verb-pron.py`,
  `scripts/polyakov-legend-to-classes.py`, `scripts/kaikki-to-classes.py`);
  never edit a tsv by hand. Lexicon files are `include_str!`-embedded:
  `cargo build` before any `xtask`. After a class-table change that
  alters output, re-import every affected part of speech.
- Two cautions from the Part 6 diary. **Never re-import the pronouns
  through Polyakov**: Alypy's lines (а҆́зъ, ты̀, и҆́же) are not its to
  produce and the merge rule cannot tell an absent line from a dropped
  one; the pronouns are re-fitted in place with `cargo xtask refit-stress
  --pos pronoun --write`. **After any re-import, run `cargo xtask
  fix-hand-alts` before `check-treebank`**: the hand overlay's `:alt`
  notes name the lexicon's alternative forms and go stale when a primary
  changes.
- Ids are stable and the game persists them: an import or a cleaning
  that would change an id is wrong until it keeps the old id (the
  citation cell is exempt from arbitration for this reason).
- Zero warnings (`cargo clippy --release --workspace`); `cargo test
  --release` green in all three crates.
- Hand annotation is done with the analyzer's readings in front of you,
  one verse at a time, linted with `narrow-hand` at 0 findings, and never
  by a script that chooses.

## Part 1 — the residue's places (release 3.0.0)

The 1,344 lists are what no paradigm names. The Part 6 diary found that
the two largest verb blocks are one missing place, and that seven
numerals are a missing class. Decide each here; what is left after this
part is residue by definition and is recorded as such.

1. **The place before the suffix.** In `stress.rs`, `Place::Pre`
   (written `P`): the last vowel of the stem the class extended.
   `resolve` takes the vowel count of that stem beside `stem_vowels` and
   `total_vowels`; `compose` in `inflect.rs` knows it, because the `ext:`
   derivation is what appended the suffix (for a stem that is derived
   and extended — `6=ext:ем:ov` on the -ova- classes — the pre-extension
   stem is the derived one, so `P` lands on the present stem's ꙋ:
   возревнꙋ́емъ). `Paradigm::parse` accepts `P` wherever it accepts `L`;
   `Place::Pre` on a cell whose stem has no extension resolves as `L`
   (state this in DESIGN.md and test it). Then name in `stress.tsv` only
   the shapes the Part 0.1 census shows (the present passive participle
   on `P` — и҆зго́нимъ — under `b` and under `a`; the -ova- participles;
   nothing the census does not show), with exemplar and count, and run
   `cargo xtask refit-stress --pos verb --write` (the lines' own forms,
   no form changed, no re-import). Gate: verb lists fall by at least
   what the census predicted for `P`; every form of every refitted line
   prints the same (the command's own check); ids unchanged; the
   consistency test and the OCS exemplar test green; zero mismatches
   after `fix-hand-alts`.
2. **The -надесѧть numerals are a compound, not a host with an
   enclitic.** первыйна́десѧть carries its one stress on the second
   element; `encl=надесѧть` was wrong because the enclitic rule (3.0)
   now says the enclitic never carries the stress, which is exactly what
   these seven lines contradict with a full override list. The proper
   model is the one the crate already has for the phonological word,
   turned round: the inflected ordinal is the proclitic and на́десѧть the
   stressed host. Give the stems column `tail=на́десѧть` (a stressed
   solid tail: the class inflects the first element, `compose` appends
   the tail with its own stress, the first element's stress is not
   written, the number mark and the ї rule see the whole word), the
   analyzer reads it apart, and the seven lines lose their overrides and
   their `encl=`. If the tail's stress needs a place of its own, it is
   an index counted from the tail's first vowel, never from the
   compound's. Gate: the seven lines carry no override that the class
   now prints; their Polyakov variants (пѧтоена́десѧте×1) stay variants;
   every -надесѧть token of the Bible analyses as before or better
   (count from the treebank before and after); the consistency test

3. **The 61 `adv-of=` adverbs.** From the Part 0.2 census: where the

## Part 3 — the lexicon's twins (release 3.1.0)

The several-lexeme share is the honest size of homonymy only if every
several-lexeme token is really several lexemes. The 2.3 census named
гдⷭ҇ь, а҆́зъ, ва́мъ and мнѣ̀ as the commonest, and they are one word each:
the lexicon holds two lines that print the same because two sources
gave them and the merge rule keeps both (the Part 6 fix made it keep
another source's lines; it does not yet recognise them as the same
lexeme).

1. **The rule of identity.** In the importer's merge (`write_outcome`
   and its neighbours in `import/`), two lines are one lexeme when they
   have the same part of speech and the same lemma under the manuscript
   fold (jers, accents, the titlo, the wide letters folded — the
   tagger's `fold_word` is the definition), and their classes print
   identical sets for every cell both attest; a line whose forms are a
   proper subset of the other's is the same lexeme with fewer
   attestations. The surviving line is the one whose id a consumer may
   hold: the older id (the game's lemma-keyed calls go through
   `slavonic.rs`; grep it for every id it names before deciding), and
   the other line's provenance joins `src` (`P:… A:…`), its counts the
   variants' weights, its note the survivor's note. A genuine pair —
   во́лна wool against волна̀ wave, a noun against a proper name with its
   own class — is not a twin and is never merged: the rule is
   mechanical and the census's list of pairs is reviewed by hand before
   `--write`.
2. **The nomina sacra.** гдⷭ҇ь against госпо́дь, бг҃ъ against бо́гъ: the
   titlo row (`lexicon/titlo.tsv`) is the print's abbreviation of one
   lexeme and the lexicon must hold one line, the full lemma, whose
   forms print abbreviated where the print does (stage 4). If two lines
   exist because a source lemmatised the abbreviation, the merge above
   folds them; if the abbreviated form is not reachable from the full
   line's paradigm, that is a titlo finding first, fixed in the tsv's
   generator or the crate, never by keeping the second line.
3. **The pronouns.** Alypy's and Polyakov's lines for the personal
   pronouns and the relative (а҆́зъ, ты̀, и҆́же, мнѣ̀, ва́мъ): merged by hand
   into Alypy's ids under the rule of 1 — `refit-stress`, never a
   Polyakov re-import — with Polyakov's counts as weights.
4. Gate, before and after: the homonymy census's twenty commonest
   surfaces contain no twin; the several-lexeme share with the tagger
   off falls from 5.6% (record the with-tagger share too); one-cell and
   set shares rise by what the twins released; zero mismatches after
   `fix-hand-alts`; held-out recall unchanged in every row; ids the game
   names unchanged; `score-disambiguation` precision still 100% for the
   rules and the tagger's precision recorded (a merged twin removes a
   choice the tagger used to make; the number may move either way).

## Part 5 — the structure the flat tree lacks (release 3.2.0)

What the 2.3 layers left is syntax a one-token window does not see: the
nominative against the accusative of an inanimate, and the clause's one
subject. The constraint layer resolves 45.0% and never excludes a hand
cell; a clause-level rule is the next elimination, and it needs a
chunker one level above the `np`/`pp` of 2.3.

1. **The clause.** In `treebank/disambiguate.rs`, a deterministic
   clause chunker: a verse is split at finite verbs and at the
   coordinating and subordinating conjunctions of the closed lexicon
   (`conj` lines, и, же, ꙗ҆́кѡ, є҆гда̀ …), each chunk holding at most one
   finite verb; a chunk the rules then contradict is not formed (the
   2.3 principle). Structure is read from the tree where the hand
   overlay has it and from the chunker elsewhere.
2. **The rules, each an elimination that names itself.** `one-subject`:
   inside a clause with a finite verb and a leaf that is unambiguously
   nominative and agrees with it, another leaf whose set is nom|acc
   drops the nominative; `verb-object`: a clause whose verb is
   transitive by the lexicon's note (`tran`, already on the verb lines)
   and whose subject is fixed drops the nominative from a following
   nom|acc inanimate; `neg-gen`: under не the direct object's acc|gen
   keeps the genitive only where the set has it (it does not eliminate
   the accusative: the print has both; record the abstention). The
   pronoun's gender from its antecedent is coreference and is out of
   scope: say so in OPEN-DESIGNS and do not write a rule for it.
3. **Calibration, decided by measurement.** The perceptron's share is
   not calibrated (91% of choices at ≥ 0.9). Fit a calibration on OCS
   dev (never the overlay, never the Bible: the overlay is the test) —
   a one-parameter temperature or isotonic bins over the margin — and
   apply a threshold only if the overlay's precision above it is ≥ 90%
   with the coverage loss recorded; below that, record the curve and
   apply nothing. A leaf the tagger declines to choose stays a set,
   which is the honest state.
4. Gate: precision 100% for every rule on the whole Part 4 overlay;
   resolution above 45.0% with the per-rule table; the tagger's column
   falls by what the rules took; the several-lexeme share and the
   one-cell share recorded; zero mismatches; `CS_NO_DISAMBIGUATE=1` and
   `CS_NO_TAGGER=1` still give the earlier tables back.

  proposed because the diary found it, and it is kept only if the

  the rule of identity is mechanical and the pairs are reviewed by hand
  before `--write`.

  itself, and is thrown out the first time it excludes a hand cell.

  the test.
- Do not fold a tagger's choice into the analysed share.
- Do not edit a class tsv by hand; do not edit a file under `treebank/`
  by hand (the hand overlay is `data/treebank-hand/`).
- Do not publish to crates.io as part of a close without the user's
  word; tag and push, and say where the published crate stands.
