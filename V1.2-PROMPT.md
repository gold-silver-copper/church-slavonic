Run in `~/Desktop/code/church-slavonic`. Read HANDOFF-PROMPT.md first; its
standing rules are this program's rules: after EVERY part, `cargo xtask

## Part 0 — Design decisions, recorded before code

Three schema questions. Decide each, write the decision and its reason
into NOTES.md (dated), and only then build. Recommendations follow; a
different choice is fine if its reason is written down and the treebank
measurement of Part 4 is the judge.

1. **The series axis.** Recommended: the 54-cell npron row stays the
   short (nominal/pronominal) series; a lemma's `plen` series enters the
   ADJECTIVE table under its own long citation form (всѧ́кїй, ѻ҆́вый,
   таковы́й, всѧ́ческїй), exactly as `adjective_series_lemmas` already
   splits an adjective entry — the long declension IS the adjective's,
   and the lift inverts adjective lemmas today. The npron key is the
   short citation form (всѧ́къ, ѻ҆́въ). Record the mapping rule in the
   extractor doc comment so both keys are derivable, never guessed.
2. **The reflexive.** Recommended: append a six-cell reflexive block to
   the personal matrix (arity 90 → 96, cells 90..95 by case; nominative
   blank by rule) and a facade `reflexive(case, recension)`; leave
   `Person` untouched (the verb schema shares it). Alypy §47's third
   column and Polyakov's себѐ feed it.
3. **The clitic cell.** Recommended: a clitic block appended after the
   reflexive (cells 96..): 1sg dat/acc (мѝ, мѧ̀), 2sg dat/acc (тѝ, тѧ̀),
   reflexive dat/acc (сѝ, сѧ̀), 1pl acc (ны̀), 2pl acc (вы̀) — the cells
   Alypy §47 prints as alternatives — with a facade
   `pronoun_clitic(person|reflexive, number, case, recension) ->
   Option<&str>` (`None` where the language has no clitic; the third
   person's short forms are already the primary row). Polyakov's `clit`
   tag then routes to a cell instead of a rank-10 variant. OCS rows get
   the same cells (ми, мѧ, ти, тѧ, си, сѧ, нꙑ, вꙑ — the UD/Syntacticus
   mappers already see them; today they demote to variants).

Also decide and record: how a print witness outranks a civil-я
transliteration (Part 1), and that `+`-headwords whose second element is
«же» are admitted by the OCS rule's wrap (то́йже, кто́же, что́же) while
every other `+` headword stays rejected.

Gate: NOTES entry; no code.

## Part 2 — Synodal non-personal pronouns: rule, sources, rows

1. **The rule, recension-aware.** `core::npron` gains a Synodal branch in
   Synodal letters and accents: the hard series (то́й, ѻ҆́нъ, ѻ҆́въ,
   и҆́нъ, са́мъ, є҆ди́нъ, толи́къ, є҆ли́къ, кі́й …), the soft series
   (се́й, мо́й, тво́й, сво́й, на́шъ, ва́шъ), the mixed ве́сь, всѧ́къ
   (short), the relative и҆́же as the anaphor + же, кто̀/что̀ (singular
   only, чесѡ̀/чесогѡ̀ as attested variants, not rule), the ни-/нѣ-
   compounds and the же-wrap. Accents follow `accent.rs`' conventions
   (the -гѡ̀ genitive against the -го̀ accusative is the registry's
   pron:genitive-accusative condition spelled in letters; the plural
   mark «̑» on direct-plural cells that would read singular — всѧ̑,
   своѧ̑ — is the adjective rule's existing mechanism). What the rule
   cannot predict, the table stores; what no source attests, the rule
   answers — never the other way round.
2. **Sources.** Admit Polyakov APRO (declinable only) and non-personal
   SPRO with `brev`→npron / `plen`→adjective routing per Part 0; admit
   Alypy §48's tables (index 0 кто̀/что̀, 1 кі́й, 2 на́шъ, 4 ѻ҆́въ; the
   толи́цы fragment only if its number/case grid is parseable without
   guessing). Homographs by accented citation form as everywhere
   (ѻ҆́нъ APRO twice in Polyakov: two entries, two observations).
3. **Registry.** Every OCS/Synodal divergence the new rows expose gets
   its `npron:` condition (сь/се́й, тъ/то́й, вьсь/ве́сь, къто/кто̀,
   чьто/что̀, the -ѥго/-егѡ̀ genitive letter, the levelled dual) —
   `check-registry` is the gate that every condition is visible cell by
   cell in the rule, as `pronoun.rs`' registry test does today.
4. `lemmas(NonPersonalPronoun, Synodal)` enumerates the new keys
   (sole-`_n` included — the wave-2 fix must hold here too).

Gate: accuracy 100/0 on the new Polyakov npron and Alypy npron sources
(README gains their rows); the README's bare-primary table reports how
much of Polyakov's npron mass the RULE reproduces without a stored cell
(that number is this part's honest measure of the rule; write it in
NOTES, whatever it is); zero warnings.
