# The Ponomar library, licence

The texts under this directory are fetched by `scripts/fetch-ponomar.sh`
from https://www.ponomar.net/maktabah/index.html (the Slavonic Computing
Initiative's library of liturgical texts: 28 books in Unicode Church
Slavonic, typeset from Synodal editions of 1906–2002). The maintainer of
the library gave the author of this repository, a friend, licence to use
all of them for this project (2026-09-06). They are not edited here:
fetched, pinned by hash in `MANIFEST.tsv`, and read.
