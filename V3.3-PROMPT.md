## Standing rules

- The lexicon holds lexical facts; the class tables hold paradigms; the
  crate holds rules that are true of the language (the ї rule, the uk,
  the phonological word); the importer holds what a source's
  transcription hides and the Bible decides (the ligature, the stress
  twins). A form a class produces is never stored; a rule that removes
  nothing is not kept; a typography rule that needs morphology is not a
  typography rule (the ligature was tried in the crate and withdrawn).
- Gate on measured numbers, never on self-consistency. Every number is
  printed by `cargo xtask eval`, `build-treebank`,
  `score-disambiguation`, `tagger-curve`, an import report or a census;
  record before and after, and a number that went down with its reason.
- The round-trip invariant: `build-treebank` at zero mismatches over
  34,470 verses; every auto-lifted leaf names every cell that prints its
  token; a narrowing carries `:by` and is scored against the overlay
  outside the invariant; a rule that ever excludes a hand cell is fixed
  or removed, never tuned; nothing that enters a tree is guessed.
- Ids never move. The importer restores them (`restore_ids`,
  `data/twins.tsv`); a re-import from the committed files must change
  no id — check with the diff before writing (the `ids removed / added`
  script of the 3.1 diary), and grep the game's `slavonic.rs` before any
  lexeme goes.
- The library depends on `unicode-normalization` only.
- Class tables are generated (`scripts/legend-adj-verb-pron.py`,
  `scripts/polyakov-legend-to-classes.py`, `scripts/kaikki-to-classes.py`);
  never edit a tsv by hand; the hand-editable files are
  `lexicon/stress.tsv`, `lexicon/titlo.tsv`, `lexicon/syn/pronouns.tsv`
  and a line with `H:` in `src` (the import never touches it).
- Lexicon files are `include_str!`-embedded: `cargo build --release`
  before any `xtask`. After a lexicon change: `cargo xtask fix-hand-alts`,
  then `cargo xtask build-treebank` — the stored treebank goes stale
  before it goes wrong; `check-treebank` is the assertion on a rebuilt
  treebank. Never re-import the pronouns through Polyakov
  (`refit-stress --pos pron --write`, or a hand line). After a Polyakov
  re-import that changes primaries, `census forms --write` and a second
  re-import let the arbiter see the new prints.
- `export RUSTC_WRAPPER=` (empty) in every shell that runs cargo in this
  checkout while the shared sccache daemon lacks Desktop access; do not
  stop the daemon, another project's build uses it.
- Hand annotation is done with the analyzer's readings in front of you,
  one verse at a time: `cargo xtask hand-draft <book> <chapter>` gives
  the draft, `scripts/hand-apply.py list` numbers its decisions,
  `scripts/hand-apply.py apply` writes the overlay from a decisions file
  (a cell, `<id> <cell>`, `abbr "<prefix>" <id> <cell> [alt n]`,
  `w "<surface>" [:lemma … :case …]`, `noalt`/`alt n`); `narrow-hand` at
  0 findings before a commit. Book indices are zero-based in the pinned
  Bible's order (Genesis 0, Leviticus 2, Psalter 21 — fragments, not
  chapters —, Isaiah 27, Luke 52, Romans 62, 1 Corinthians 63).
- Zero warnings (`cargo clippy --release --workspace`); `cargo test
  --release --workspace` green.

## Part 0 — the census of the verbatim (release 3.3.0)

Every part below starts from a number. Add `cargo xtask census
verbatim`, run it on the rebuilt treebank, record in NOTES. Gate: the
census runs and its numbers are written down; nothing else moves.

1. Every verbatim leaf of the treebank (`(w …)` without `:amb`),
   grouped by why the lexicon does not print it, in this order of
   tests: (a) **found by key, not exact** — `Lexicon::analyze` returns a
   reading with `exact false`: bucket by the letters that differ between
   the surface and the reading's print (ѧ against ꙗ at the head, ѷ
   against ѵ, ѡ against о inside the word, ї against і, a jer, a stress
   position, more than one), with the twenty commonest surfaces per
   bucket and the lexeme each names; (b) **a titlo token with no row** —
   the token carries U+0483 or U+2DED-style superscripts and the titlo
   index has nothing: by prefix, with counts (нн҃ѣ, прⷪ҇ро́чество,
   а҆́гг҃лскими, воскрⷭ҇нїе, спⷭ҇нїе…), and how many of them the lexicon
   would print if a row existed (the full form is in the lexicon); (c)
   **no reading at all**: by the surface's ending and capitalisation —
   names (capitalised, most of them once), numerals, the -надесѧть and
   -десѧть compounds, everything else — with the hundred commonest
   surfaces. Print the three totals against 35,731.
2. `cargo xtask census homonymy` for the verbs: the `:amb` tokens whose
   readings hold exactly one finite-verb reading beside readings of
   another part of speech (ви́дѣ: the aorist beside the locative of
   ви́дъ; глаго́ла, положѝ, речѐ-type), by the other reading's cell —
   how many are a bare locative, a bare vocative, a genitive singular —
   and how many tokens. This is Part 3's material.
3. `cargo xtask score-disambiguation` as the baseline (3,377 leaves,
   rules exclude none, tagger 75.62%), and the count of the overlay's
   verbatim leaves with `:lemma` (the gold's own list of what the
   lexicon lacks).

## Part 1 — the print's last letters (release 3.3.0)

What Part 0.1(a) and (b) count is typography and the titlo table, not
the lexicon, and it goes first because Part 2 measures against it.

1. **The izhitsa's kendema.** In the print ѵ read as a vowel carries the
   kendema (ѷ) when unstressed and the oxia when stressed; ѵ read as [v]
   (after а, е) carries nothing: кѷмва́лъ, є҆гѵ́петъ, є҆ѵа́гг҃лїе. A stage-4
   rule beside the ї rule in `Form::print` (`apply_izhitsa_rule` is the
   place): the letters keep ѵ, the print writes ѷ by position; the
   analyzer folds (`comparison_key` already does). Measure with the
   census bucket before and after; the consistency test proves the
   lexicon's ѵ-forms still round-trip (a stored print with ѷ that the rule
   now writes itself is dropped from the override, as the ї rule's were).
2. **The head ѧ҆ against ꙗ҆.** The print spells some lexemes with
   initial ѧ where Polyakov writes ꙗ (ѧ҆зы́къ 156 tokens beside ꙗ҆зы́къ
   1,034; the census says which others). Decide by the numbers: where a
   lexeme's Bible prints are all ѧ҆, the lemma's letters are ѧ (the
   importer with the Bible as arbiter, the way the ligature is written —
   a letter of the lexeme, not a rule); where both spellings occur for
   one lexeme, both are the lexeme's (the minority as a letter variant
   the arbiter may now write, with its count) — extend the arbiter to a
   letter variant of the citation form only when the Bible prints it and
   the id stays (`id_lookup_key` folds ѧ/ꙗ at the head like the uk). Ids
   never move: verify with the diff.
3. **The titlo rows.** From Part 0.1(b): a row per abbreviation the
   Bible uses for a lexeme the lexicon holds (нн҃ѣ = ны́нѣ, the closed
   adverb; прⷪ҇ро́к- = проро́к-; а҆́гг҃л- = а҆́ггел-; спⷭ҇- = спас-; воскрⷭ҇-
   = воскрес-; …), in `lexicon/titlo.tsv` by hand with the Bible count
   in the row, the titlo index rebuilt, the lifter re-run. A titlo
   token that abbreviates a lexeme the lexicon lacks waits for Part 2.
4. Gate: the census bucket (a) falls by what the two rules explain, (b)
   by the rows added — record each; `build-treebank` at zero mismatches
   and its verbatim count down; the consistency test green; the hand
   overlay's `(w "нн҃ѣ")`-type leaves re-drafted into leaves (`narrow-hand`
   0); the game's strings unchanged or re-pasted as findings.

## Part 2 — the lexicon's gaps (release 3.3.0)

Part 0.1(c) and the gold's `:lemma` list say what is missing. Each gap
is a class question first and a line second.

1. **The ū-stems.** любы̀ (любвѐ, любо́вь…), це́рковь/це́ркви, кро́вь,
   свекры̀, бꙋ́квы: what class table row prints them today (N-something

2. **The numerals.** три, четы́ре, пѧ́ть … де́сѧть, сто̀, ты́сѧща and the

3. **The verbs and adverbs the gold named**: пови́тъ (the aorist of

## Part 3 — the verb that is several lexemes (release 3.4.0)

The clause rule stays silent where the verb is `:amb` (ви́дѣ, 3.2's
finding). The proper tool is an elimination over readings that names
itself, like every rule before it.

1. **bare-loc.** A locative in Church Slavonic is always governed by a
   preposition; a reading whose every cell is locative, with no
   preposition before it within the clause (the chunk may cross an
   adjective, never a verb or punctuation), is impossible and is
   eliminated (`reduce` for an `:amb` token, `narrow` for a set). Then
   ви́дѣ is the aorist and one-subject fires. Check the exceptions the
   grammar knows before writing it (the locative of time without a
   preposition? Alypy's chapter) and let the overlay decide.
2. **bare-voc**, the same shape: a reading whose every cell is vocative,
   in a clause with no interjection and no imperative, is eliminated
   (voc-drop does this for sets; extend it to `:amb` readings), and
   **a genitive singular beside a nominative subject**: not a rule
   (record why: the genitive has too many uses).
3. Gate: precision 100% on the overlay for each rule or the rule goes;
   the several-lexeme share and the tagger's column fall by what the
   rules took (record); one-subject's resolution rises (Genesis 1:4);
   zero mismatches; `CS_NO_DISAMBIGUATE=1` still gives the 3.2 table
   back.

## Part 4 — the tagger's transfer, measured (release 3.4.0)

The tagger is 89% on OCS and 76% on the overlay; the overlay is the test
and the Bible is never trained on. What can be known without breaking

1. **The fold's blind spots.** From `score-disambiguation`'s error
   kinds (nominative/accusative 126, another feature 59, gender 51,
   genitive/accusative 46), sample thirty of each with the OCS training
   material's nearest analogue: is the error a Synodal convention the
   fold does not reach (the nomina sacra as neighbours, the ѹ/ѿ letters
   the fold now sees, по with the locative), or syntax no window sees?
   A convention the fold can reach is a fold fix in
   `crates/church-slavonic-tagger` (`fold_word`), retrained
   (`train-tagger`, the model's hashes recorded), measured on OCS dev
   and the overlay.
2. **What Synodal gold would buy: a measurement, not a model.** Five-fold
   over the overlay's chapters (train on four, test on one, the OCS
   material included), reported as a number in NOTES and OPEN-DESIGNS 1b
   — the shipped model stays trained on OCS only. If the number is far
   above 76%, the open design says what a Synodal training source would
   have to be (not the Bible, not the overlay: another edition, the
   Menaion?) and stops there.
3. Gate: the OCS dev number not lower; the overlay number recorded

## What not to do

- Do not put morphology in the typography stage (the ligature lesson);
  do not put a source's transcription in the crate (the importer, with
  the Bible as arbiter).
- Do not change an id; do not re-import the pronouns through Polyakov;
  do not run `check-treebank` on a stale treebank and call it a bug.
- Do not write a rule that selects; every rule eliminates, names itself,
  and goes the first time it excludes a hand cell.
- Do not train or calibrate on the Bible or the overlay; the five-fold
  of Part 4 is a measurement and ships no model.
- Do not add hand lines for the names the guesser can carry; do not add
  a numeral class without its paradigm source.
- Do not edit a class tsv or a file under `treebank/` by hand.
