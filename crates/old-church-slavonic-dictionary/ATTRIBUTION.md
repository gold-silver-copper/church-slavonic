# Data attribution

The bundled dictionary senses are derived from the English Wiktionary Old
Church Slavonic edition through Wiktextract and Kaikki. They are redistributed
under CC BY-SA 4.0. The exact dump date, extraction date, byte count, checksum,
and extraction revision are recorded in the crate's bundled `SOURCE.toml` and
in the workspace's `data/dictionary/source.json`.

Wiktionary contributors: https://en.wiktionary.org/

Kaikki machine-readable OCS dictionary:
https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Old%20Church%20Slavonic/

Wiktextract: https://github.com/tatuylonen/wiktextract
