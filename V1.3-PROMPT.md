## The diagnosis

- **[verified] The print's ї is a positional rule, and it is
  exceptionless.** Over 739,896 Bible tokens: an UNSTRESSED non-initial і
  before a vowel or й is written ї in 31,490 tokens and і in 2 (both
  apparatus tokens, «꙾І҆ѡсі́а»); a STRESSED і before a vowel stays і in
  6,181 tokens (люді́й 511, сі́и 435, дні́й 368, кі́йждо 302, ті́и 245,
  дрꙋгі́й) and is never ї; a word-initial і stays і (11,108; ї initial
  17, all apparatus variants). The crate folds ї to і unconditionally
  (`orthography.rs`: the sources spell і in 2,388 cells and ї in zero — a
  transliteration convention of Polyakov and Alypy, not the print's). The
  remaining ї is lexical: 3,204 tokens of ї before a consonant in
  loanwords (кївѡ́тъ 135, вїна̀ 118, вїно̀ 107, вїногра́дъ, фарїсе́є,
  пїла́тъ) — no rule, no source spells them; they stay out. Note what
  the rule says about layering: it depends on STRESS ("unstressed і"),
  so even this letter convention is decided after the stress is known.
- **[verified] What that rule is worth.** Of the 128,497 verbatim tokens
  left after v1.2, folding ї→і on the token lifts 23,677 (3,734 distinct

  прїи́де 504, мꙋ́жїе 376, прїидо́ша 323, бж҃їѧ 283, прїи́детъ 262, бж҃їй
  235, бра́тїѧ 222, ски́нїи 207 … — 18.4% of the band, 3.7 points of the
  whole Bible, and се́й's whole feminine and neuter. The hand overlay's
  тве́рдїю, но́щїю, подо́бїю, бы́лїе are in it.
- **[verified] The ѡ and є classes are not the same kind of thing.**
  Folding non-initial ѡ→о lifts 1,667 tokens, but the top of the list is
  сѡ́тъ 230, ма́лѡ 123, мно́гѡ 111, ско́рѡ 89, є҆ли́кѡ 46, ра́нѡ 35,
  зако́ннѡ 29: the print's -ѡ marks the ADVERB against the adjective's
  neuter -о, and a fold would produce FALSE analyses. There is no adverb
  part of speech; this class is a different design and stays verbatim.
  Folding non-initial є→е lifts 540 (и҆ноплемє́нницы 84, колєсни́цы 54,
  ста́рєцъ 28, конє́цъ 18): the print's plural є-widening where the rule
  placed the kamora or nothing — a STRESS-COUPLED letter (Alypy §6: the
  wide letter and the kamora are the same mark, chosen by the stress

- **[verified] The variant rows already spell 21,342 verbatim tokens.**
  The lift inverts bare keys only; a surface a `_n` row spells never
  lifts. 2,694 distinct verbatim surfaces are stored variant forms: бѣ̀
  855 (бы́ти_4's aorist), ни́мъ 730, немꙋ̀ 524, ни̑мъ 353, негѡ̀ 328, нѧ̀

  домꙋ̀ 334, рѣ́ша 290, дѣла̀ 266, сы́ны 264, своего̀ 222, и҆́де 214, рцы̀
  154 … — 16.6% of the band. The facade resolves a `_n` key today
  (`base_lemma`), so a leaf `(n до́мъ_2 :case gen :num sg)` renders and
  round-trips by construction.
- **[recorded] Most stored Synodal cells are stress, not letters.** The
  accent-pattern token (`s<N>`/`e`, one per row) collapsed the
  stem-stressed and end-stressed rows (v0.7–v0.9); what stayed stored
  is ~6.5k rows whose stress is mobile or whose stress couples with a
  print letter convention (the kamora, the wide ѡ/є, the final varia),
  against ~8.7k rows that differ from the rule in LETTERS (animacy
  explains 1,020 outright; the rest are stems, fleeting vowels, the
  variant spellings). A row cannot say "the letters are the rule's, the
  stress is the exception" today: it stores the whole form. That is why
  a per-cell stress fact from the Bible has nowhere to land.
- **[verified] The per-cell primary is unmeasured because of the same
  gap.** v1.2 recorded (and the treebank refuted, twice) that a
  lemma-keyed row's bare key is the lexicographic sort's choice among
  attested rows: Polyakov's counts are per FORM, so at a bundled cell the
  nominative's frequency wears the accusative's tag («га́ды» over
  «гадѡ́въ»). The dictionaries DO attest stress per cell (every Polyakov
  and Alypy form is accented); what they cannot attest is which reading
  the print USES at a cell, nor the number marks their bundles conflate.
  The print counts per cell — once the treebank's analyzed leaves name
  the key that produced them, which needs the variant inversion first.
  The analyzed slice is 136,136 tokens today.
- **What the Bible cannot be.** A paradigm source: running text carries
  no lemma and no cell. Every form that enters a table still needs a
  source that names its cell — a dictionary, a grammar, a hand-curated
  witness line — or the treebank's unambiguous analysis of it, which is
  the crate's own output and therefore can rank but never add.

## Part 0 — What "accuracy against the Bible" means (design, recorded)

Write into NOTES.md, dated, before code. The Bible enters in three roles
and no other, and the crate's Synodal form becomes two layers.

1. **Typography authority.** A convention the print applies without
   exception over the whole text (measured, with the count and nature of
   its exceptions) belongs in `orthography::realise`, the canonical
   Synodal typography, like the initial ѻ/є and the psili. It applies to
   table keys, table cells, rule output and the sources' forms alike, so
   the tables regenerate consistently and accuracy is unaffected by
   construction. Write the threshold for "exceptionless".
2. **Witness pool.** As since v1.1; unchanged.
3. **Frequency oracle over the crate's own vocabulary.** The treebank's
   unambiguous analyzed leaves, once they name the key that produced
   them, give a per-(lemma, cell, form) print count that may RANK — which
   row holds the bare key, which stress a cell carries when the sources
   disagree — and nothing else: it adds no form, never outranks a
   witness, and an ambiguous token contributes nothing. Its file is
   generated by `check-treebank` from the pinned Bible and committed
   (`data/treebank-freq.tsv`), so a refresh is reproducible without the
   gitignored treebank.
4. **The stress layer — the architecture decision.** A Synodal form is
   LETTERS composed with STRESS. The letters come from the rule and the
   tables accent-blind — a skeleton that still carries the number-mark
   intent (`^`, the marker the rule already emits for a dual/plural cell
   spelled like a singular) but no oxia/varia/kamora and no widened ѡ/є.
   The stress layer is applied on output: the lemma's accent pattern
   (the token the row already carries), plus per-cell exceptions (a
   stress fact cell listing the cells whose stressed vowel differs from
   the pattern — the mobile paradigms), plus the mark-as-grammar cases
   the stress position decides (the kamora or the wide ѡ/є on a `^`
   cell, the word-final varia, the monosyllable's plural varia «и҆̀хъ»,
   the unstressed-ї rule of Part 1). This is where the accent-pattern
   token already points; making it the whole story means the tables
   store what the rule path already computes. It is the design the
   frequency oracle needs, because per-cell stress is exactly what the
   print attests per usage and what a dictionary's bundle conflates.
   Record the invariant that keeps this honest: composition(letters,
   stress) reproduces every attested form BYTE-FOR-BYTE — accuracy
   stays one number at 100.00%, and gains two diagnostic numbers
   (letters-only accuracy, stress-only accuracy) that show where each
   layer's rule is wrong.

Also record what stays out: the -ѡ adverbs (an adverb layer is its own
design), the loanword ї, the enclitic accent contexts.

## Part 1 — The print's ї (typography, stress-aware)

`realise` for Synodal writes ї for an UNSTRESSED non-initial і that
precedes a vowel or й, і otherwise; ї before a consonant is left as
typed (the loanword class is lexical; say so in the doc comment). The
fold must be a fixed point on the print's forms: assert it in the
orthography tests over the ї and the stressed-і cases (сїѧ̑ / сі́и, лю́дїе
/ люді́й, прїи́де, кі́йждо, бж҃їй). `comparison_key` keeps ї and і one
letter. Because the rule reads the stress, an UNACCENTED input keeps і
(the layering of Part 3 will place the stress first and the ї after;
until then the realised print forms carry their own stress and the rule
has what it needs). Then `refresh-data`: table keys change where the
rule applies (`syn:всѧ́кій` → `syn:всѧ́кїй`; `syn:кі́й` stays, stressed),
and every cell with them; prove with a test that both spellings of a
lemma resolve (`npron("всѧ́кій", …)` equals `npron("всѧ́кїй", …)` —
`fold` realises input already). Rebuild the treebank; lift the hand
overlay's ї leaves.

## Part 2 — The variant keys in the lift (syntax-side)

The index inverts every published key, not only the bases: for each base
lemma probe `lemma_2`, `lemma_3`, … until a key stops resolving (the
facade answers an opaque suffix by rule on the whole string — detect
that, never index it). An analysis carries the key that produced the
surface; when the bare key and a variant produce the SAME surface for
the same cell, the bare key wins and the variant is not a second
analysis (no false ambiguity from duplicated cells); when only a variant
produces it, the leaf is `(n до́мъ_2 :case gen :num sg)`. The linter
accepts `_n` lemmas. Rebuild and measure: the coverage delta, the
per-family delta for the prepositional н- forms and бы́ти's бѣ̀, and a
new column the check prints — analyzed tokens by variant key — the
provenance the frequency file needs. `check-treebank` writes
`data/treebank-freq.tsv` (recension, pos, key, cell, form, count) from
unambiguous analyzed leaves, sorted, committed; nothing reads it yet.

Gate: zero mismatches; the crate untouched; the hand overlay re-lifted
where a variant now serves (бѣ̀ ×2 in Gen 1).

## Part 3 — The stress layer

The refactor of Part 0's decision 4, in the proper layers, gated by the
composition invariant at every step:

1. **Schema.** Each Synodal row keeps its accent-pattern cell and gains a
   STRESS-EXCEPTION fact cell: a compact map from cell index to the
   stressed-vowel index (and the mark kind where the position does not
   decide it), for the cells whose stress the pattern does not give.
   Fact cells hold derived tokens, never forms (the schema's rule).
2. **Extractor.** For every attested Synodal form, invert the print:
   letters = the form with its marks stripped and its widened ѡ/є
   un-widened where the rule's skeleton has о/е under a `^` (a lexical
   ѡ stays a letter); stress = the stressed vowel's index and the mark
   kind. Subtract the letter layer against the rule's skeleton
   (accent-blind), the stress layer against the pattern token; store
   only what each layer cannot predict. The `^` intent is the rule's,
   never inferred from the source. The transliteration folds of v1.2
   (ꙗ/ѧ, the monosyllable's mark kind and presence, the number mark on
   pronoun rows) become statements about the STRESS layer of a
   transliterated source, which is what they always were.
3. **Runtime.** `resolution` composes: letters (own cell, bare cell,
   rule) then stress (exception cell, pattern token, lemma), then the
   typography (`realise`, now including Part 1's ї). One copy.
4. **Measure.** Accuracy is still one number per source (100.00%/0 by
   the invariant); the harness gains two diagnostic rows per source —
   letters-only (compare after stripping marks and un-widening) and
   stress-only (compare the stressed-vowel index and mark kind on
   letter-equal forms) — and the README's bare-primary table is
   re-read in that light. Report the table sizes before/after (the
   ~6.5k stress-coupled rows are the expected collapse) and the
   treebank: zero mismatches, coverage unchanged by construction.

Gate at every commit: accuracy 100.00%/0; registry (the dead-weight audit
learns the two layers); witnesses; zero mismatches; the v1.2 ledger
tests untouched.

## Part 4 — The print ranks what the sources conflate

The extractor reads `data/treebank-freq.tsv` as a RANKING source: (a) in
`assign`, after soft/primary/noise, a lemma's candidate rows are ordered
by the print count of their forms before the lexicographic signature;
(b) at a cell where two sources attest different STRESS for letter-equal
forms (Alypy's «ра́ба» beside Polyakov's «раба̀», the 112 slots the
refresh reports), the print count decides which stress the bare row
carries and the other becomes the exception of a variant. Nothing else
changes: no form is added, blanked or moved; a witness's primacy stands;
a lemma without print counts sorts as before. Measure, both directions:
the bare-primary table before/after and a new number, "bare agreement
with the print" (the share of analyzed treebank tokens the BARE key
produces); the treebank at zero mismatches with the variant-key column
shrinking; the refuted v1.2 cases as tests (`га́дъ`'s «гадѡ́въ» on the
bare key by print count, `сꙋ́дъ`'s «сꙋда̀», `ра́бъ` unchanged); and the
reproducibility rule — `refresh-data` with and without a built
`treebank/` gives byte-identical tables, since the committed tsv, not
the build, is the source.

## Known honest edges (do not "fix" casually)

- The loanword ї (кївѡ́тъ, вїно̀): lexical, unattested by any source;
  verbatim by design until a source spells it.
- The -ѡ adverbs: no adverb part of speech; a fold is a false analysis.
- The enclitic accent contexts («закленꙋ́ тѧ», «Ѻ҆ни́ же»): a
  token-sequence rule, out of scope by design — even under the stress
  layer, which is per word.
- The ambiguous band (31.0%, larger after Part 1): its disambiguation is
  its own future design with its own invariant. Do not smuggle it in.
- Frequency ranks, never adds: if a Part-4 result ever needs a form the
  tables do not hold, that form's home is a witness row or a dictionary
  cell, never the tsv.
- Letters are not fully accent-blind and never will be: the wide ѡ/є
  and the kamora are one mark chosen by the stress; the letter layer
  carries the `^` intent and the stress layer realises it. Say this in
  the schema docs so nobody "simplifies" it away.

The finish line: the print's typography is the crate's typography where
the print is consistent; a Synodal form is letters composed with a
stress the crate can measure on its own; every stored variant the print
uses lifts under its own key; the bare keys and the cells' stress agree
with the print by count, measured; and the next map — the adverb layer,
бы́ти's future, the ambiguous band — is a list of designs, not
conventions.
