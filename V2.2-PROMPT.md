# V2.2: the six designs after 2.1

The prompt for church-slavonic 2.2.0, 2.3.0 and 3.0.0: the six open
designs of `docs/OPEN-DESIGNS.md` and `HANDOFF-PROMPT.md` (after
`v2.1.0`), in the order their dependencies dictate. Read `docs/DESIGN.md`,
`docs/OPEN-DESIGNS.md`, `HANDOFF-PROMPT.md`, the "2.1.0" entry of
`CHANGELOG.md` and `V2.1-PROMPT.md`'s postscript first. Execute the parts
in order; each part ends with its gate measured, a CHANGELOG entry under
the release it belongs to, a dated NOTES.md entry (decisions and
findings, including what was rejected and why), and a commit. Three
releases: 2.2.0 after Parts 1–3, 2.3.0 after Parts 4–5, 3.0.0 after Part
6 (it changes how every Synodal line reads). Each release re-tests the
game (`~/Desktop/code/vertograd`) and is tagged.

## Standing rules

- The lexicon holds lexical facts; the class tables hold paradigms; the
  crate holds rules that are true of the language. A form a class
  produces is never stored; a stem a class can derive is never stored;
  `cargo xtask census stems` is the arbiter, and removing lines is the
  measure of a derivation's success.
- Gate on measured numbers, never on self-consistency. Every number is
  printed by `cargo xtask eval`, `check-treebank`, an import report or a
  census this prompt adds; record before and after, and record a number
  that went down with its reason. A Kaikki or Polyakov reproduction
  number is a reproduction number: when a source's form is wrong, say
  so, count it, and keep the language's form as the primary.
- The round-trip invariant: `check-treebank` at zero mismatches over
  34,470 verses, and every auto-lifted leaf names every cell of its
  lexeme that prints the token. Nothing that enters a tree is guessed:
  syncretism is the set, homonymy is `:amb n`, a narrowing carries the
  rule or model that made it and is scored against gold outside the
  invariant.
- The library depends on `unicode-normalization` only; anything that
  learns from a corpus lives in the tools crate or a new crate.
- Class tables are generated (`scripts/kaikki-to-classes.py`,
  `scripts/legend-adj-verb-pron.py`, `scripts/polyakov-legend-to-classes.py`);
  never edit a tsv by hand. Lexicon files are `include_str!`-embedded:
  `cargo build` before any `xtask`. After a class-table change that
  alters output, re-import every affected part of speech, or the
  consistency test fails.
- Zero warnings (`cargo clippy --release -p church-slavonic -p
  church-slavonic-tools`); `cargo test --release` green in both crates.
- Hand annotation is allowed and is the most valuable thing in this
  prompt; it is done with the analyzer's readings in front of you, one
  verse at a time, linted, and never by a script that chooses.

## Part 0 — the censuses

Every part below starts from a number. Add the commands, run them, record
in NOTES.md. Gate: each census runs and its number is written down.

1. `cargo xtask census verb-cells --ocs`: for every OCS verb class, the
   aorist, imperfect, l-participle and past-participle cells the table
   holds against what the class's Leskien type predicts (Part 1 defines
   the prediction); the share of Kaikki cells that agree with the type,
   disagree, or are absent; the UD variants (`U:`) on verb lines whose
   cell is one of these blocks, by cell — this is what Part 1 stands to
   reproduce.
2. `cargo xtask census closed`: the 2,504 closed lines by note tag
   (`adv` 2,293, `advpro` 99, `conj` 36, `pr` 29, `part` 18, …); for the
   adverbs, how many an adjective already in the lexicon would produce
   with the neuter short form (-о, printed -ѡ) or the locative (-ѣ), and
   how many of those print with the wide ѡ in Polyakov; for the
   prepositions, which cases each governs in the Bible, counted from the
   auto-lifted treebank (the noun leaf after `(f …)`, its set
   intersected with a case) — the empirical case frames Part 2 writes
   down.
3. `cargo xtask census clitics`: every Bible token that ends in an
   enclitic written solid (же, бо, ли, ми, ти, сѧ, мѧ, тѧ, ны, вы, си, и҆̀)
   and is not a lexeme with `encl=`; how many analyse whole, how many
   analyse as host + enclitic once the host's final varia is read as an
   oxia (Землѧ́же → землѧ̀ + же), how many neither. This is Part 3's gate.
4. `cargo xtask census homonymy`: the 37,621 `:amb` tokens of the
   treebank by shape — several lexemes of one part of speech, several
   parts of speech, a closed-class word beside an inflected form — and
   the twenty commonest surfaces; and the underspecified sets by size.
   This is Part 4's material.
5. `cargo xtask census stress`: every Synodal stress column with an
   exception list (`a{…}`, `b{…}`, `c{…}`: 395 noun lines today, plus
   adjectives, verbs, pronouns), the lists normalised to their shape
   (which cells are S, E, L, which alternative), the shapes ranked by
   frequency, and how many lines the twelve commonest shapes would
   absorb as named paradigms. This is Part 6's gate, and it is run now
   so the number is on record before anything else moves.

## Part 1 — the non-present verb cells by type (release 2.2.0)

The present is by Leskien type since 2.1; the aorist, imperfect,
l-participle and past-participle cells of the OCS verb classes are still
Kaikki's majority, and Kaikki's tables are template output with junk
there (коснѫти: косехъ, кослъ; клѧти: кльнхъ, кльнлъ; грѧсти: грѧдлъ).

1. In `scripts/kaikki-to-classes.py`, extend `type_stem` and the type
   table so each Leskien type declares the stem and the ending set of
   the aorist, the imperfect and the l-participle, as the grammar has
   them: the sigmatic aorist on vowel stems (`1-хъ`, second and third
   person `1-` bare: дѣлахъ, дѣла; хвалихъ, хвали), the -ох- aorist on
   consonant stems (`2-охъ`, `3-е` for velars with the first
   palatalisation, `2-е` for dentals: несохъ, несе; рекохъ, рече;
   грѧдохъ, грѧде), class II with -нѫ- kept first and the root aorist as
   the alternative (двигнѫхъ | двигохъ), the nasal types on the
   infinitive stem (клѧхъ, клѧ); the imperfect on the stem the type
   names (дѣлаахъ, хвалꙗахъ, несѣахъ, речаахъ — the husher rule of 2.1
   writes речаахъ from `3-ѣахъ`); the l-participle on the infinitive
   stem (дѣлалъ, неслъ, коснѫлъ, клѧлъ, реклъ). Where Kaikki's majority
   disagrees with the type, the type's cell is the primary and Kaikki's
   is not an alternative; the census of Part 0.1 lists every such cell.
2. Reseed; re-import `kaikki --pos verb --write` then `ud --pos verb
   --write`; rebuild; `census stems --pos verb --ocs`; `eval`.
3. Synodal: read the Part 0 stem census for the 90 `1=бі`-type lines
   and the нн/н lines; where a class-level statement exists (the
   ї-stem's cells whose ending opens with a vowel; the long past
   passive's нн where the short has н), put it in
   `scripts/legend-adj-verb-pron.py` and re-import the three Synodal
   verb sources; where it is Polyakov's own variation, say so in NOTES
   and leave the line.
4. Gate, before and after: UD dev+test verb recall ≥ 90.59% and
   Syntacticus ≥ 94.91%, both expected to rise (the aorist and
   l-participle are the largest remaining verb misses; print the miss
   census with `CS_RECALL_BLOCKS=1` before and after); the number of `U:`
   variants on OCS verb lines in these blocks falls (record it); the
   Kaikki verb reproduction number is recorded even if it falls, with
   the count of cells that are Kaikki's junk against the type; OCS
   stored stems stay ≤ 56; the OCS exemplar test extended with one
   aorist, one imperfect and one l-participle per type; zero warnings,
   tests green.

## Part 2 — the closed-class lexicon: government, prosody, the adverb cell (release 2.2.0)

The 2,504 closed lines are a flat list with a note tag. Government is a
lexical fact and the linter's ten-preposition table is the wrong home
for it; prosody is a lexical fact and Part 3 needs it; the adverb from
an adjective is a cell.

1. **Subcategory as class.** The closed lexeme's `class` column (today
   `0`) becomes its subcategory: `prep`, `conj`, `part`, `adv`, `advpro`,
   `intj`, `pred`. The note keeps what it has. A class table
   `classes/closed.tsv` exists only to name the subcategories and their
   one cell (`word`); the parser accepts it.
2. **Government.** On a preposition line the `stems` column carries
   `gov=<case>[|<case>…]` in the order of frequency from the Part 0.2
   census, every preposition of the Bible covered (the census lists
   them); a case that the census attests but the grammar (Alypy's
   preposition chapter) does not know is recorded as a variant frame
   with its count in the note, never silently. `Lexeme::government()
   -> &[Case]`. The linter's `PREP_CASES` is deleted and the linter reads
   the lexicon; its tests move to lexicon-backed ones.
3. **Prosody.** `pros=encl` on the enclitics (же, бо, ли, ꙋбо; the
   pronoun clitics carry it on their cells already by `clit`), `pros=procl`
   on the prepositions and не/ни, nothing on a tonic word.
   `Lexeme::prosody() -> Prosody { Enclitic, Proclitic, Tonic }`.
4. **The adverb cell.** On the Synodal adjective classes an `adv` cell:
   `adv=1-о^|1-ѣ` for the hard classes, the soft classes' equivalent,
   the comparative adverb beside it (`comp.adv`), the `^` making the
   print write the wide ѡ (до́брѡ, мꙋ́дрѡ) and the analyzer read it apart
   from the neuter до́бро; `AdjCell` grows a way to say the adverb (a
   `Degree`/case-less variant or a new `Cell::Adv`, decide in DESIGN.md
   and keep names round-tripping). Then the census of Part 0.2 decides
   each closed `adv` line: produced by an adjective already in the
   lexicon and printed the same → the closed line is deleted and its
   provenance moves to the adjective's note (`adv: P:` with Polyakov's
   count); produced but printed differently (Polyakov's accent or letter
   differs) → it stays, with `adv-of=<adjective id>` in its stems and
   the difference in the note; no adjective → it stays. The deleted count
   is the design's own test.
5. Re-import the closed classes (`polyakov --pos closed --write`, adding
   the subcategory, government and prosody to the importer so a
   re-import reproduces the file; the government census output is the
   importer's input, committed under `data/intermediate/`). Re-import
   the Synodal adjectives so the new cell is on every line's provenance.
6. Gate: closed-class coverage of the treebank ≥ 28.1% and the
   several-lexemes share ≤ 6.0% (an adverb cell must not create a new
   homonymy against a surviving closed line: that is what step 4's
   deletion rule prevents; count any residue); every Bible preposition
   has a frame; the linter reproduces its old findings from the lexicon;
   held-out adjective recall ≥ 89.31%; the game's adverb strings, if it
   prints any, unchanged.

## Part 3 — the phonological word (release 2.2.0)

`Form::print` decides oxia against varia inside one word; the print
writes a host and its enclitic solid (Землѧ́же) and accents the unit. This
is a fifth, optional stage, deterministic, and its census is every solid
enclitic in the Bible.

1. **The rule in the crate.** `Form::print_in(recension, &Prosodic)` (or
   a builder on `Form`) where `Prosodic` says what follows in the unit:
   an enclitic's letters, and whether a proclitic precedes. The rule:
   the accentual word is host + enclitics; the varia is the mark of the
   unit's final stressed vowel, the oxia of every other, so a host whose
   last vowel is stressed takes the oxia when an enclitic follows
   (землѧ̀ + же → землѧ́же; рабѝ + же → рабі́же with the ї rule intact);
   the number mark, the kamora, the wide letters and the monosyllabic
   varia stay word-level and unchanged; a proclitic changes nothing in
   the print (не вѣ́мъ) and is modelled only so the unit is complete. The
   2.0 `encl=` lexemes (иже, кождо, the `сѧ` verbs) are the special case
   of this rule and must produce exactly what they produce today
   (`mark_skip` becomes derived from the unit, not stored); the existing
   consistency test proves it.
2. **The treebank.** A node `(pw host encl…)`: a phonological word
   written solid, the host any leaf, each enclitic an `(f …)` with
   `pros=encl`, rendered through the rule. The lifter, for a token that
   analyses whole in no way, tries the Part 0.3 split: strip a known
   enclitic, analyse the host with its final varia read as an oxia, and
   lift `(pw (n землѧ.n :case nom|acc|voc :num sg) (f же.x))` when the
   host is one lexeme and the unit renders the token byte-for-byte. The
   linter treats a `pw` as its host for agreement.
3. **Generation.** `church_slavonic::prosody::words(tokens) ->
   Vec<PhonologicalWord>` groups a token sequence into units by the
   lexicon's prosody (an enclitic attaches left, a proclitic right), and
   `render` in the treebank and any consumer (the game's sentence
   builder) accent through it; second-position placement of an enclitic
   is a function the generator may call, not something the renderer
   does.
4. Gate: the Part 0.3 census before and after — every token that
   analyses as host + enclitic is lifted (`TokenFate::Analyzed` or
   `Underspecified` under a `pw`), Землѧ́же in Genesis 1 among them, the
   hand overlay updated and linted; zero mismatches; the 2.0 enclitic
   lexemes print unchanged (the consistency test); the game's tests
   green with its sentence output byte-identical (paste the real output
   for any string that changes, as a recorded lexicon finding).

## Part 4 — the constraint layer of homonymy, and the gold (release 2.3.0)

The 37,621 several-lexeme tokens and the 215,087 syncretic sets are what
a disambiguator narrows. The proper tool is a Constraint Grammar over the
readings the analyzer already returns, layered over the linter's rules,
never guessing, always naming its rule, scored against gold that must
grow first.

1. **The gold.** Hand-lift Genesis 2 and 3 into `data/treebank-hand/`
   (the overlay's format, fully specified leaves, `narrow-hand` at 0
   findings, the linter clean), then the first chapter of each of Exodus,
   Psalms, Matthew and John — six chapters beyond Genesis 1, chosen for
   register. Every hand cell is checked against the lexicon's set with
   `narrow-hand` before it is committed; a hand cell outside the set is
   a lexicon finding first. Record the count of hand leaves and of
   narrowings (Genesis 1 has 283 and 179).
2. **The constraints.** `treebank/disambiguate.rs`: rules over a verse's
   readings, each named, each an elimination, never a selection — an
   adjective inside an `np` drops readings agreeing with no noun of the
   phrase; a noun after a preposition drops readings outside the
   preposition's `gov` frame (Part 2); a finite verb with a nominative
   subject drops readings disagreeing in number; a closed-class reading
   beside an inflected one is not decided by any rule in this part (it
   is the tagger's); a vocative reading is dropped except before or
   after an imperative or an interjection, and only where the set has
   another member. Structure the rules read comes from the tree (the
   auto-lifted trees are flat: `np` and `pp` are formed by a small
   deterministic chunker — a preposition and the next nominal, an
   adjective and the next agreeing noun — and a chunk that a rule then
   contradicts is not formed). A narrowing writes the rule's name on the
   leaf (`:by prep-gov`, `:by np-agree`) and the set it narrowed from
   stays readable (`:from nom|acc|voc.sg`). A several-lexeme token whose
   readings a rule reduces to one lexeme becomes a leaf with `:by` and
   `:from-lexemes n`.
3. **Scoring.** `cargo xtask score-disambiguation`: over the hand
   overlay, for each hand leaf, whether the constraint layer's set
   contains the hand's cell (precision: never wrong) and how often it
   equals it (resolution); by rule; the same over the syncretic sets and
   the several-lexeme tokens of the whole treebank as coverage. The
   invariant is untouched: a narrowed leaf renders through its first
   cell as before.
4. Gate: precision 100% on the hand overlay (a rule that ever excludes
   the hand's cell is wrong and is fixed or removed, never tuned);
   resolution recorded per rule; the treebank's underspecified share
   falls and the one-cell share rises, both recorded; `:amb` tokens
   reduced to one lexeme counted; zero mismatches; the linter's own
   findings on the hand overlay still zero.

## Part 5 — the statistical tagger (release 2.3.0)

Only what the constraints leave open, and only once the gold can score
it.

1. A new crate `crates/church-slavonic-tagger` (the library stays
   dependency-free): an averaged-perceptron or HMM tagger over (lexeme
   id, cell) readings, trained on the gold morphology of UD PROIEL train
   and Syntacticus (the loaders exist in `eval.rs`), features from the
   surface under the manuscript fold, the neighbouring surfaces and the
   readings' parts of speech; model stored as a committed binary under
   `data/models/` with its training command and hashes.
2. Evaluation first on OCS: accuracy on UD dev+test over the tokens
   with several readings (the number that can go down), reported by part
   of speech. Then transfer to the Synodal Bible through the fold, scored
   on the Part 4 gold: the tagger is applied only where the constraint
   layer left several readings, its choice written `:by tagger :p 0.87`,
   and the treebank reports "disambiguated by tagger" as its own column,
   never folded into the analysed share.
3. Gate: OCS dev+test accuracy recorded; on the hand overlay the
   tagger's precision recorded honestly (it will be below 100%); a
   tagger choice never enters the treebank without `:by tagger`, and
   `check-treebank` can rebuild with the tagger off (`CS_NO_TAGGER=1`)
   and get the Part 4 numbers back; zero mismatches.

## Part 6 — the accent-paradigm inventory and weighted evidence (release 3.0.0)

Stress today is `a`, `b`, the two measured mobile paradigms `c` and `d`,
and per-lexeme exception lists (`a{gen.pl=E;…}`) on 395 noun lines and
more elsewhere. The proper description is a closed inventory of accent
paradigms with rules over cells, and the primary form chosen by evidence
with weights.

1. **The inventory.** From the Part 0.5 census, name the paradigms in
   `lexicon/stress.tsv` (the format already allows `S/E/L` per cell and
   the `sg/du/pl` shorthands): the stem and ending types, the mobile
   types where the singular and plural differ, and the finer types where
   one cell retracts (рꙋка̀ : рꙋ́кꙋ, рꙋ́ки; the vocative's retraction; the
   participle types), each with an exemplar and Alypy's or the census's
   count; a lexeme's stress column becomes a paradigm name, and an
   exception list survives only where no paradigm fits. The Polyakov
   importer's `stress_column` fits the paradigm before it writes a
   list. Re-import every Synodal part of speech.
2. **Evidence with weights.** Polyakov's counts enter the lexicon as
   weights on variants (`variants` gains an optional `×n`); the importer
   chooses a cell's primary by attestation in the print where a source's
   forms disagree (the pinned Bible decided ѻ҆́вцꙋ over ѻ҆вцꙋ̀ fourteen to
   one: make that rule the importer's, with the Bible's counts from the
   treebank as the arbiter); `Analysis` and `Reading` carry a `weight`
   and the analyzer ranks by it after exactness. The old 99% noun gate is
   struck from the record with the reason (it measured the dictionary).
3. Gate: stress columns with an exception list fall from the Part 0.5
   count to the residue the inventory cannot name (record both, and the
   twelve paradigms' absorption); Polyakov cells reproduced by the
   primary rise for nouns, adjectives and verbs; held-out recall
   unchanged or higher in every row; Bible one-reading coverage
   unchanged or higher; the game's strings byte-identical or re-pasted
   as recorded findings; the lexicon line format documented in
   `docs/DESIGN.md` and the README, version 3.0.0.

## What not to do

- Do not keep a Kaikki cell that the type contradicts as an alternative
  "for reproduction"; count it as junk and move on.
- Do not put a case frame or a prosodic class in the linter, the
  renderer or a script; they are lexicon columns.
- Do not let the phonological-word stage touch a word-level mark; the
  consistency test over the `encl=` lexemes is the proof it did not.
- Do not write a constraint that selects; every rule eliminates, names
  itself, and is thrown out the first time it excludes a hand cell.
- Do not fold a tagger's choice into the analysed share, and do not
  train on the Bible.
- Do not start Part 6 before Part 0.5's number is on record, and do not
  invent a paradigm the census does not show.
- Do not edit a class tsv by hand; do not edit a treebank file under
  `treebank/` by hand (the hand overlay is `data/treebank-hand/`).
