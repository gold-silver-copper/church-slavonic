# Institutional terms

Recorded 2026-08-27 from the maintainer's report: the maintainer's university
holds rights to (1) A. E. Polyakov's corpus-based grammatical dictionary of
Church Slavonic and (2) the Russian National Corpus Church Slavonic
subcorpus, and has granted permission for everything created from them in
this project to be released under MIT OR Apache-2.0, on the basis of a shared
goal. Derived cells from these sources therefore ship in the generated
tables under the crate licence; the raw artifacts are not committed.

(`e32844093cc173edf9241868fdad7167dfb63fb7f105d146af02a645ff382fec`) — for
