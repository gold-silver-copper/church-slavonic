## 2. Headline: gap burn-down potential

Of the gap's 51235 `unregistered-lemma` token types,
**5118 (10.0%) are covered accent-blind by some projected OCS
cell surface** (137406 OCS attested cells projected in total).
Per the accent asymmetry, every one of these is a surface-skeleton seed:
the accent fact must still come from Synodal-side evidence, and each
admission remains a reviewed, projection-seeded curated admission —
projection never bypasses review or satisfies the gold gate alone.

Caveat: "divergent" here means no candidate is attested in the Synodal
evidence. That conflates genuine divergence with plain corpus
non-attestation — the Bible does not attest every paradigm cell (duals of
proper nouns, rare locatives), so the true divergence rate is an upper
bound read from the pattern table below, not from the raw count.

The exact tier is small by construction: across ALL lexemes only
19 accent-blind-matched OCS surfaces carry a printed accent to check,
of which 19 also match a Synodal accented surface —
the accent asymmetry (OCS sources are unaccented) predicted exactly this.

## 5. Recommendation

At the decision point of docs/UNIFIED_LANGUAGE_PROMPT.md: 39.9% of OCS lexemes find a Synodal counterpart under the declared rules, 42.5% of the dually-identified lexemes' attested cells hit attested Synodal evidence accent-blind, and 5118 of the gap's 51235 unregistered-lemma token types (10.0%) are reachable by some projected OCS cell surface. The accent-blind hit rate is an under-count (every unattested-in-the-Bible cell lands in "divergent"), and the residue is not noise: the top divergence patterns are exactly the divergences the thesis predicts — dual endings, OCS instr.sg. -омь/-емь against Synodal -омъ/-емъ, uncontracted long-adjective and imperfect formations against Synodal contracted ones — i.e. a small, nameable inventory, not scattered incompatibility. That supports proceeding to phase 2: the systematic thesis holds at the orthography layer, and the residue seeds the named-divergence registry rather than refuting the model. The tempering fact is the headline: projection reaches ~10% of the gap, so the merge's burn-down payoff is real but bounded — the OCS Wiktionary lexicon (3,081 lexemes) simply covers a minority of the Elizabeth Bible's vocabulary, and every seeded admission still needs a Synodal accent fact and review before it can close a gap row.
